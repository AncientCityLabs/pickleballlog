Play pickleball.
It remembers the journey.

Pickleball Log notices when you arrive at your courts and when you leave, and turns a season of playing into something you can actually look back on — sessions, hours, courts, what it cost, what you played with, and who you played with.

It runs by itself

Pick the courts you play at. Pickleball Log starts and ends your sessions when you arrive and leave. There is no timer to remember and nothing to tap when you would rather be warming up. When it misses one — and it sometimes will — you can start, correct or add a session by hand.

It stays on your iPhone

There is no Pickleball Log account, because there is no Pickleball Log server. Your sessions, photos, notes, spending and the people you played with are stored on your device and backed up with your normal iPhone backup. We cannot read them, because they are never sent to us.

The full detail is on the privacy page, including the two places the app does talk to Apple.

The part worth paying for is the arithmetic

Recording sessions is free and always will be. What costs money is the layer on top: what an hour of pickleball actually costs you, whether your membership is paying for itself, what your paddle has cost per hour of play, who you have played with most, and how a season compares with the one before.

Not on the App Store yet

Pickleball Log is being built and tested on real courts. This page exists so that the privacy policy, support and terms are where they should be before anything ships.
