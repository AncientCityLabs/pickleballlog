Cancelling ends access to the paid statistics. It never touches your sessions, photos, notes or any other record — those are on your device and stay there, readable and exportable, whether or not you ever pay.

What the app does not promise

Pickleball Log detects sessions using iOS geofencing. It is accurate but it is not perfect: sessions will occasionally be missed, recorded with the wrong times, or recorded when you were only passing. Detection also stops entirely if you force-quit the app, which is an iOS behaviour outside our control. Every session can be added, corrected or deleted by hand for exactly this reason.

Do not rely on Pickleball Log as your only record of anything that matters. Export your data periodically if it is important to you.
